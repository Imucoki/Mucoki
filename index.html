Simple Home Gold Tests (No Machine Needed)

These tests work in Kenya, USA, UK, UAE, India, Canada, and Australia. Test before you buy.

🧲 Magnet Test

Pure gold is not magnetic. If your jewellery jumps to a magnet, it's fake or contains other metals. Get a strong magnet from your kitchen or fridge. That's all you need.

Difficulty: Super Easy

⚖️ Weight & Volume Test

22K gold has a density of 17.5 g/cm³. Weigh your gold. Measure its volume using water. Do the math. If it matches, it's real. If not, it's fake.

Difficulty: Medium

✅ Hallmark Check

Look for "22K", "916", or "917" stamps. These prove karat weight. Use a magnifying glass. If there's no hallmark or it's unclear, be suspicious. Real gold always has clear marks.

Difficulty: Super Easy

👃 Skin Reaction Test

Genuine 22K gold doesn't cause skin reactions or discoloration. Wear it for 24 hours. Check for green marks or irritation. Real gold = clean skin. Fake gold = green marks.

Difficulty: Super Easy

💧 Acid Test

Professional nitric acid test is most accurate. Visit a certified shop for this test. It costs a little but gives you 100% certainty. Worth it for expensive pieces.

Difficulty: Professional Only

🔊 Sound Test

Drop your gold on a hard surface. Real gold produces a unique dull thud sound that lasts 1-2 seconds. Fake gold produces a quick, high-pitched ring. Listen closely. You'll hear the difference.

Difficulty: Easy
